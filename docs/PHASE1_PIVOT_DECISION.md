# Phase-1 Pivot Decision

**Status:** recommendation for human review; no automatic pivot has been executed. Validation and test remain unopened.

## V1 result

AETHER Positive-Control Substrate V1 established a deterministic attack-to-message-change-to-receiver-failure chain on 16/16 development cases. Whole-message rescue and attacked-message induction were both `1.0`. The mediator-to-outcome BVP edge reached Level 1. V1 remains immutable.

## V1 hardening result

Coarse hidden-state replacement produced strong development causal responders, including a randomly selected late-block control. None of six refined candidates survived the frozen shuffled-source specificity criterion. The hardening checkpoint is `303e0d8`. Because every V1 clean case had label A, the shuffled-clean comparison could not distinguish label-level representation from generic overwrite; this motivated V2 rather than invalidating internal localization by itself.

## Balanced V2 result

V2 fixed 40 diverse deterministic cases: 24 development, 8 validation, and 8 test, balanced within every split. Only development executed. The first preserved smoke exposed decoder-only right-padding and changed mediator wording; V2.1 corrected those execution defects before any causal scan and was separately frozen.

On all 24 V2.1 development cases, A→B had 12/12 clean competence and 8/12 attack success. B→A had 9/12 clean competence and 12/12 attack success. There were no invalid mediator outputs. The full eligible intersection was 8 A→B cases and 9 B→A cases. On those eligible cases, whole clean-message rescue, attacked-message induction, and decision-field replacement were each `1.0` in both directions.

## Why internal localization was not evaluated

The predeclared behavioral gate required reliable competence and attack behavior in both classes before causal search. B-class clean competence was only 75%, and A→B attack success was 66.7%. Proceeding only on the 17 eligible examples would create outcome-conditioned selection and asymmetric denominators. Therefore the coarse scan, semantic activation controls, candidate classification, and hierarchical head search were stopped—not treated as negative internal-localization results.

The generation-step mechanism and scientifically valid Qwen pre-`o_proj` head access were implemented and accepted technically. They were not used to generate causal candidate claims because the substrate gate failed.

## Claims that remain supported

1. V1 supports a real-model causal serialized-message mediator under its positive-control substrate.
2. V2 confirms bidirectional message causality on every eligible development case.
3. The current model/task pairing does not provide a sufficiently symmetric balanced substrate for an unbiased internal search.
4. No case-specific, label-level, or head-level internal mechanism is supported or rejected by V2; those classifications are `INSUFFICIENT` because the scan was not permitted.

## Candidate Phase-1 pivot options

1. Narrow Phase 1 to the heterogeneous causal-mediation methodology and report the internal-localization feasibility boundary, with V1/V2 as transparent positive-control and falsification evidence.
2. Replace the underpowered 0.5B task/model pairing with a separately predeclared, competence-qualified open model, subject to compute and deadline review.
3. Use a non-medical deterministic symbolic trust task with balanced labels, declared as a new substrate rather than mutating V2.
4. Move full internal localization to Phase 2 and make Phase 1 a calibrated message-mediation and instrumentation paper.

No option is selected here. Human review must choose the scientific scope before new experiments.

## Recommendation

**PIVOT PHASE 1.** Do not open validation/test and do not run another unbounded activation search on the current substrate.
